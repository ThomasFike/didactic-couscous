FAQ

Helpful answers for guests attending our wedding ceremony and reception.

What is the dress code?

Semi-formal. The ceremony will be outdoors in the grass. The weather is normally in the 60s during the day and in the 30s overnight.

Is there parking available?

There is a lot attached to the EagleVail Pavilion that can fit up to 40 cars. The ceremony and reception will be at the same location.

What time should I arrive at the ceremony?

The ceremony starts at 4 so please allow time to get parked and seated prior to the start of the ceremony.

How do I RSVP and what is the RSVP deadline?

Click on the RSVP tab and submit the form. All RSVPs are due by August 21st.

Can I take photos?

We ask that you refrain from taking photos during the ceremony. After the ceremony, you are welcome to take photos during the cocktail hour and reception.

We will be using the app “PhotoCircle” where anyone can upload the photos you take for everyone to see. Use the link below to join the album ahead of time:

https://join.photocircleapp.com/SP9JWH7D3R
