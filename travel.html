Travel Information

Getting to Avon, Colorado for the wedding

Travel & Local Planning

Everything you need for flights, driving, hotels, and nearby activities around the wedding weekend.

Flight Info (Prefer DEN)

Denver International Airport (DEN) is the closest major airport and the preferred arrival point for guests. It is approximately 2 hours from Avon, and offers the most flight choices from across the U.S.

Recommended airlines with frequent service to DEN include United, Southwest, Delta, Sun Country, and American. Once you land, rental cars and shared shuttles are available at the terminal.

Drive Info

From Denver, take I-70 W through the mountains to Exit 171 for Avon. The drive is scenic but can be snowy in September, so check road conditions before traveling.

If you are driving from the airport, allow 2 to 2.5 hours. From the venue, Vail Village is a quick 15-minute drive, while Beaver Creek is about 25 minutes away.

Hotel & Airbnb Info

Avon and nearby Vail Valley have a range of hotels and vacation rentals. Recommended areas include Avon, Eagle-Vail, Vail Village, and Beaver Creek.

Airbnb Recommendations:

Search for vacation rentals in Avon or Eagle-Vail for the most convenient access to the venue. Look for properties with mountain views and modern amenities. Popular areas include:

  • Avon town center (walking distance to some restaurants)
  • Eagle-Vail area (quiet and scenic)
  • West Vail area

Things To Do
